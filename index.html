How it works

Six steps from dry land to running water

Most residential projects run start to finish within one to two weeks, depending on depth and access.

1

Site Assessment

We walk the land and discuss your water needs and budget.

2

Geological Survey

Resistivity survey identifies the best drilling point.

3

Drilling

Rig is mobilised and drilling begins to target depth.

4

Casing & Testing

Borehole is cased and yield-tested for consistency.

5

Pump Installation

Submersible pump and, where needed, solar setup fitted.

6

Handover

Water quality test, walkthrough and maintenance guide.
