About G-Tech Drilling

Survey first. Drill once. Water for good.

We are a Harare-based drilling crew who believe a borehole is only as good as the geological read behind it. Here is how we got here and how we work.

12+ Years drilling across Zimbabwe
Our story

Built on one idea: guess less, survey more.

G-Tech Drilling started with a simple frustration: too many boreholes across Zimbabwe were sited by guesswork, leaving families and farms with dry holes and wasted budgets. We set out to do it differently. Every job begins with a proper geological survey, not an educated guess.

We run our own drilling rigs and keep the same crew on a project from the first site walk through to final handover, so nothing gets lost between contractors and nobody passes the blame when something goes wrong.

Today we work across Harare and the surrounding provinces for homeowners, farms, schools, lodges and small businesses who need water they can rely on through the dry months.
